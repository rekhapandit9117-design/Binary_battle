FAQ Center

1. How to add cash?

Go to Wallet → Click Add Cash Choose amount → Pay via UPI Balance instantly update ho jata hai.

2. How to join match?

App open karke Mode select karo (CS/BR/LW/1V1). Entry fee pay karo, match join ho jayega.

3. ID & Password kab milta hai?

Match start hone se thoda pehle milta hai. Yeh "My Matches" section mein show hota hai.

4. How to withdraw money?

Wallet → Withdraw par click karein. UPI ID enter karo → Amount select → Submit.

5. Withdraw kitne time me aata hai?

Usually instant ya few hours (within 24h). Kabhi bank issue se thoda delay ho sakta hai.

6. Match ID/Password nahi mila?

Support team se contact karo. Ya next match join karo (refund policy applicable).

7. Is Binary Battle safe?

Yes, yeh ek secure platform hai. Fair play aur active support hamesha available hai.

8. Fake proof / abuse ka kya hoga?

Fake payment or abuse = Direct Account Ban.

9. Support kaise contact kare?

Telegram: @BinaryBattleCare Ya app ke contact us section me se contact karein.

10. Refund kab milta hai?

Agar match issue hua ho (Host/ID/Pass issue). Refund wallet me credit ho jata hai.
